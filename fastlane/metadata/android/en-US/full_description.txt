<p><b>Your phone as a piece of paper.</b></p><p>Scratchpad Launcher is a minimal Android home screen split in two: the top half is a plain text box that's always there, so you can write something down without opening any app. The bottom half is a small, fixed list of your most-used apps. Nothing else.</p><p><br><b>FEATURES:</b></p><ul><li><b>Persistent scratchpad:</b> Text survives reboots, is stored locally, and is excluded from Android backups so it never leaves your device.</li><li><b>Minimal app list:</b> A handful of apps, no icons, no clutter.</li><li><b>Customizations:</b> Resize text, rename apps, hide unused apps, show or hide the status bar, app text alignments, etc.</li><li><b>Gestures:</b> Double tap to lock screen. Swipe left or right to open apps. Swipe down for notifications.</li><li><b>Privacy:</b> No data collection, no accounts, no tracking. FOSS Android launcher, licensed GPLv3.</li><li><b>Launcher features:</b> Dark &amp; light themes, dual apps support, work profile support, auto app launch.</li></ul><p><b>Accessibility Service:</b> Used exclusively to let you turn off your phone's screen with a double-tap gesture. It is optional, disabled by default, and doesn't collect or share any data.</p><p>This is a fork of <a href="https://github.com/tanujnotes/Olauncher">Olauncher</a>, a minimal ad-free launcher by tanujnotes, with the app grid cut down and a scratchpad put in its place.</p>
